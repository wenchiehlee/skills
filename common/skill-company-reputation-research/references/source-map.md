# Source Map

Use this reference to select channels and search patterns. Do not load every source mechanically; choose the channels that match the company's geography, language, industry, and role.

## Taiwan Channels

- GoodJob 職場透明化運動: interview, salary, work experience, labor-rights oriented content.
- 面試趣: interview process, interview questions, interview rating, salary links.
- 比薪水: salary, benefits, role-level compensation, workplace experience.
- 求職天眼通: anonymous workplace warnings, labor-law discussion, company reputation.
- 518 好公司: company reviews, salary, interview discussion.
- Dcard 工作板: current anonymous discussions and jobseeker anecdotes.
- PTT: Salary, Tech_Job, Soft_Job, Oversea_Job, and industry-specific boards.
- Mobile01 and other forums: use only when surfaced by targeted search results.
- Government/public records: company registration, labor-law violation announcements, court records, MOPS, TWSE, TPEx.

## Global Channels

- Glassdoor: employer reviews, interview reports, salaries.
- Indeed company reviews: reviews, ratings, interview notes.
- Blind / TeamBlind: employee discussion, culture, compensation, layoffs; stronger for tech and large companies.
- Levels.fyi: compensation, especially tech roles.
- Comparably: culture and compensation signals.
- Fishbowl: professional community discussion.
- Reddit: employee or candidate anecdotes; search relevant subreddits.
- LinkedIn: company page, headcount trend, public employee posts, hiring pattern.
- Layoffs.fyi, TheLayoff, WARN notices, or local equivalents: layoff signals.
- Regional sources: OpenWork Japan, Jobplanet Korea, JobStreet/SEEK, AmbitionBox India, Kununu Europe.

## Credibility Heuristics

- Stronger: official public records, court/administrative records, repeated firsthand accounts across independent platforms, specific posts with role/location/time context.
- Medium: anonymous reviews with specific details, multiple similar interview reports, salary entries with role and seniority.
- Weaker: vague complaints, single viral posts, reposted screenshots without source, anonymous accusations without time/place/role.
- Suspicious: sudden clusters of generic positive reviews, repeated wording across platforms, ratings inconsistent with detailed review text.

## Red-Flag Taxonomy

- Compensation: below-market pay, unpaid overtime, opaque bonus, delayed salary, misleading total compensation.
- Workload: chronic overtime, on-call expectations, weekend work, unrealistic headcount.
- Management: abusive behavior, public humiliation, high-pressure sales, unclear decision rights.
- Hiring: bait-and-switch role, fake salary range, repeated interviews without feedback, request for sensitive personal data too early.
- Legal/labor: labor-law violations, unpaid severance, contractor misclassification, non-compete misuse.
- Stability: layoffs, hiring freezes, repeated restructuring, executive churn.
- Ethics/safety: harassment claims, unsafe work environment, misleading products, compliance issues.
